# Prompy

> **The Ultimate Secure, 100% Offline AI Prompt Manager for Android.**

---

## Core Philosophy

Prompy is designed for **power users** who need a centralized, organized, and—most importantly—**private** way to manage their library of AI image prompts. Unlike other managers, Prompy operates on a **zero-cloud policy**, ensuring that your creative intellectual property never leaves your physical device.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Robust Prompt Management

- **Creative Suite:** Effortlessly create, edit, and categorize prompts with titles, detailed descriptions, and custom tags.
- **Visual Context:** Attach reference images to any prompt to maintain a visual history of your AI generation styles.
- **Rapid Workflow:** One-tap **Quick Copy** functionality with haptic-style visual feedback for seamless use with external AI tools _(Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion)_.
- **Organization:** Clean grid-based layout with intelligent card previews showing prompt content and associated tags.

---

### 2. Advanced Security & Privacy — *The "Secure Vault"*

- **Biometric Integration:** Optional fingerprint lock to protect the entire application upon launch.
- **The Secure Vault:** A secondary, PIN-protected partition within the app specifically for your most sensitive or private prompts.
- **Dual-Layer PIN System:** Independent security credentials for App Entry fallback and Vault access, allowing for granular security control.
- **100% Offline Integrity:** Hardcoded protection that blocks all automated cloud backups (Google Drive) and data extraction attempts. If you delete the app, your data is gone—just as true privacy should be.
- **Native Auth UI:** Custom-built numeric keypad and visual dot-indicators for a modern, secure authentication experience.

---

### 3. Smart Search & Discovery

- **Instant Search:** Real-time, dynamic filtering across prompt titles and content.
- **Multi-Tag Filtering:** A powerful bottom-sheet filter menu that allows you to narrow down your collection by multiple tags simultaneously.
- **Intelligent Sorting:** Organize your library by **Newest**, **Oldest**, or **Alphabetically (A–Z)**.

---

### 4. Data Portability & Encryption

- **Encrypted Backups:** Export your entire prompt library or just your vault to **AES-256 encrypted ZIP files**.
- **Custom Passwords:** Choose between your Vault PIN or a completely custom password for your backup files.
- **Universal Import:** Easily restore your library from previous ZIP exports, maintaining all images and tags.

---

### 5. High-End User Experience

- **Dynamic Theme Engine:** Fully customizable primary color accents with built-in presets and a granular color picker.
- **Triple-Mode Appearance:** Supports **Light** mode, a refined **Dark** mode, and a high-contrast **AMOLED Black** mode for battery saving and eye comfort.
- **Fluid Motion:** iOS-style "rubberband" bounce scrolling and smooth slide-in screen transitions.
- **Adaptive UI:** System status and navigation bars dynamically adapt their colors to match the app headers and active theme.
- **Physical Navigation Support:** Native support for the Android **Back** gesture to dismiss menus, modals, and screens.

---

## How It Works

Prompy leverages Android's local storage and shared preferences to manage data **without a backend server**. By utilizing a custom-built **Material 3** design language and a double-frame rendering system, the app achieves a high-performance, fluid feel that matches the most modern system applications.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> All prompt data—including images—is stored in a **secure, private local database** that is inaccessible to other applications on the device.
